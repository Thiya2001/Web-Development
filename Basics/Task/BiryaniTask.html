Mutton Biryani

Ingredients :

  • Mutton
  • Rice
  • Onions
  • Curd
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • Spices
  • Ghee

Procedure :

  1. 1 kg (or about 2.2 lbs)
  2. About 750g to 1 kg
  3. Sliced and often fried (called birista or fried onions)
  4. Used for marinating the mutton
  5. A key flavor base
  6. Whole spices like cloves, green and black cardamom, cinnamon, and bay leaves. Ground spices include coriander, cumin, red chilli powder, and garam masala.
  7. Clarified butter, used for frying and added for richness.
